Prices

Every price except the Big Mac is editable. Change one and every chart follows. Edited cells turn gold. Tags show where each price came from.

Exchange rates: ECB euro reference rates (published around 16:00 CET each business day), crossed through the euro; currencies the ECB does not publish come from ExchangeRate-API daily market rates. Refreshed every morning. iPhone: iPhone 17 starting price on each country's apple.com store (euro area = apple.com/de). Spotify: Premium Individual monthly price on each country's spotify.com page (US excludes sales tax, Argentina excludes local taxes). Switch 2: Nintendo's list price for the base console after the September 2026 increases; Japan is the Japanese-language, Japan-only model at ¥59,980. IKEA Billy: white 80×28×202 cm bookcase on each IKEA country site (regular price, not member or sale prices). Latte: tall caffè latte; official in Japan and Taiwan, gift-voucher face value in Korea, estimates elsewhere. Taxi 5 km: a 5 km daytime ride with no waiting; Tokyo computed from the regulated tariff, other cities are estimates for a metered taxi or ride-hail fare. Big Mac: local prices from The Economist July 2026 survey (github.com/TheEconomist/big-mac-data), valued at today's exchange rates; the history chart uses the survey-date rates. US prices exclude sales tax; most others include VAT, which the iPhone basket largely measures. GDP-adjusted values fit each basket's dollar price against GDP per person and compare each currency with its fitted fair price.
